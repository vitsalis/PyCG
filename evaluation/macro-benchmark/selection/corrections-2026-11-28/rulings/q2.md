# Q2. jsonpickle: per-dtype and per-offset fan-out

Adopted by the lead in place of a referee, maintainer's instruction of
2026-11-28. Read at `c7fc8fb3`. Paths are relative to
`evaluation/macro-benchmark/`; a package's files are cited from its root
under `projects/<name>/`, its ground truth is under `ground-truth/`, and
`benchmark-rules.md:<n>` is a line of `evaluation/benchmark-rules.md` at
that commit.

**1. The question.** About 190 outside edges of jsonpickle name each
`numpy.dtypes.*DType` class and each pandas offset class one by one. Is
that fan-out intended?

In `jsonpickle.ledger` the per-class names are site-callee entries:

| Family | Entries | At declared open receiver sites |
|---|---:|---:|
| `numpy.dtypes.<X>DType.newbyteorder` and kin | 198 | 198 |
| numpy scalar classes (`numpy.float64.tolist`, `.item`, ...) | 90 | 90 |
| pandas offsets and `_libs` classes (reducers, `__setstate__`, reconstructors) | 63 | 63 |

The largest sites: `pickler.py:610:48` `obj.__reduce__()` (38 such
entries), `pickler.py:695:41` `obj.__getstate__()` (36),
`ext/numpy.py:100:50`, `188:54`, `293:54` `obj.dtype.newbyteorder('N')`
(27 each), `ext/numpy.py:52:50` (25), `153:47`, `343:60` (22 each),
`unpickler.py:587:36` `stage1.__setstate__(state)` (22), `583:23`
`f(*args)` (15). Of jsonpickle's 928 outside pairs, 467 are at the 38
sites that `jsonpickle.md` "Open receiver sets" lists.

**2. The ruling.** **Apply rulings (e) and "Open receiver sets"; no
change.** The fan-out is the receiver-class naming rule applied to a
large admitted receiver set. It does not cover sites outside the declared
list; a per-class name at an undeclared site needs its own witness as any
edge does.

Effect: none on the ground truth (0 edges). The outside row "without
jsonpickle" that the ruling requires is not in the published results;
Q1's reporting covers it.

The audit of these corrections found one entry of the first family that
cannot run, `numpy.dtypes.ObjectDType.newbyteorder` at
`ext/numpy.py:153:47`, an error outside this question; it is removed
(`jsonpickle.md`, "Corrections of 2026-11-28"). The counts above are those
at `c7fc8fb3`.

**3. What the rules say.**

- Micro and macro naming: "A method of an outside object is named by the
  class of the receiver, also when that class inherits it" (Ground-truth
  conventions, "Callees outside the package",
  `benchmark-rules.md:1874-1878`).
- "One name per admitted concrete receiver class (jsonpickle)", After
  Codex audit round 1 (e) (`benchmark-rules.md:2559`): "The rule ... has
  no exception for a large receiver set, and B4 does not replace known
  concrete receivers by their defining base. The numpy scalar classes and
  the `numpy.dtypes` classes that can arrive at a site are named one by
  one".
- "Open receiver sets of a generic serialisation path (jsonpickle; the
  lead, 2026-11-22 ...; reported to the maintainer)"
  (`benchmark-rules.md:2568`): the callees at these sites are "one name
  per concrete library class that a handler can forward ... the graph at
  these sites is a LOWER BOUND", and the outside edges "are read for all
  ten packages and for the nine without jsonpickle".

The rules decide it: rulings (e) and "Open receiver sets" name exactly
this fan-out, and the second was reported to the maintainer when made.
Every entry counted above lies at a declared open receiver site.

**4. The evidence.**

- `jsonpickle/ext/numpy.py:52`, `100`, `188`, `293`: `obj.dtype` of an
  ndarray or numpy scalar is an instance of a `numpy.dtypes` class (numpy
  1.25 and later make each dtype an instance of its own `DType` class;
  `numpy.dtypes` is a public module), and `newbyteorder` is inherited
  from `numpy.dtype`: by the receiver-class rule each concrete class is a
  name.
- `jsonpickle.md` "Open receiver sets" records that the set was verified
  in the numpy 2.5.4 and pandas 3.0.6 sources and that Cython-generated
  reducers and older releases leave it open (reasons 1 to 3).

**5. Against.** Naming by the defining base (`numpy.dtype.newbyteorder`)
would give one edge per site instead of 20 to 40, and a tool that
resolves the method correctly to the base is scored as missing every
concrete name. That is a convention change for all packages (the
micro-benchmark names an inherited method through the class that the
code names, the macro-benchmark through the receiver class); ruling (e)
considered and rejected it for jsonpickle, and `same_function.json`
cannot join the names because they are different classes.
